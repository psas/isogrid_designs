# Isogrid Designs
Repository for PSAS Isogrid tutorials and designs.

## Tutorials
Isogrid was developed in the late 60s/early 70s by NASA and it's contractors. NASA released a handbook for designing isogrid structures, [NASA CR-124075 Isogrid Design Handbook](https://femci.gsfc.nasa.gov/isogrid/NASA-CR-124075_Isogrid_Design.pdf). The tutorials in this repo aim to make the examples in the handbook more accesible

## Isotanks
Aluminium isogrid tanks are the current baseline for PSAS's Launch Vehicle 4 (LV4) as they are light-weight, easily manufacturable and compatible with liquid oxygen (LOX). Other options were considered were:
* Composite tanks - Manufacturing LOX compatible composite tanks is difficult for student groups and operationally risky.
* Thin-wall aluminum monocoque tanks require specialty welding services

PSAS isotanks are constructed from a thick walled, extruded aluminum pipe that has an isogrid pattern machined onto the outer surface. Thicker flanges are left on the ends of the pipe to allow the end cap to be welded in place without requiring specialty welding services. 

### Pathfinder Tanks
Pathfinder tanks were manufactured at LV3.1 scale (6" ID) and installed on the Minimal Test Stand (MinTS).

## Isomodules
#### Pathfinder Modules
After intial success with the pathfinder isogrid tanks, use of isogrid for the airframe modules was investigated. Two closed-web isogrid modules were first flown on LV3.1, Launch 14 during May 2026. An open-web isogrid module was designed as part of the '25-'26 Airframe Capstone for use on LV3.2.
